About PlaceMellon

PlaceMellon gives you Carnegie Mellon photos and artwork at whatever size your mockup or prototype calls for.

How It Works

PlaceMellon picks a photo with similar proportions: landscape for wide sizes, portrait for tall ones and any photo for sizes close to square. It crops the photo to fit and keeps the part with the most detail and color.

  • A size and category select a consistent photo. The selection can change when the source collection changes. Choose random photos to vary it on each request.
  • An exported image works on its own. Save the file to keep it with your project. An image loaded from a PlaceMellon address depends on the service.
  • SVG artwork stays sharp at any size. Scotty, bio placeholders and solid colors are drawn as vector shapes. PNG exports are also available.
  • Source photos aren't saved to disk. PlaceMellon fetches each one from CMU Selects when it's needed.

Contributing to PlaceMellon

PlaceMellon is a TypeScript app built with Express. It fetches photos from CMU Selects and crops them with sharp. Scotty, bio placeholders and solid colors are drawn as SVG; sharp turns them into PNGs. Tests check sizes, cropping, photo selection and the options.

The GitHub repository has the code, its tests and documentation. To report a problem or suggest a change, open an issue or send a pull request.
